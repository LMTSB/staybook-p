# Competitor Landscape — SpacialStay

**Research date:** 23 November 2026  
The products below overlap with parts of SpacialStay. They are not all identical competitors: some are booking marketplaces, while others supply the 360°/3D tour technology that SpacialStay could integrate.

## 1. Booking marketplaces — direct competitors for hotel bookings

| Company / product | Why it competes | Strategic implication |
|---|---|---|
| [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/) | Large accommodation marketplace with hotel, apartment, and alternative-stay inventory. | Competes on supply, reviews, trust, loyalty, and conversion. |
| [Agoda](https://www.agoda.com/) | Strong Asia-Pacific accommodation marketplace and especially relevant to Thailand/SEA travellers. | Important regional benchmark for pricing, mobile experience, and hotel supply. |
| [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/) | Accommodation marketplace focused heavily on homes and distinctive stays. | Competes on unique spaces, host content, and guest experience. |
| [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/) | Global OTA with hotels and travel bundles. | Competes on reach, package deals, loyalty, and distribution. |
| [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/) | Major travel platform with strong Asia coverage. | Competes on regional travel ecosystem and accommodation choice. |
| [Traveloka](https://www.traveloka.com/) | Southeast Asian travel platform with accommodation and travel services. | Relevant regional competitor for Thai and SEA customer acquisition. |
| [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels) | Hotel discovery, reviews, and comparison; booking journey can route through partners. | Competes at the research and trust-building stage. |

## 2. 360° / 3D tour products — adjacent competitors and potential suppliers

| Company / product | What it offers | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| [Matterport](https://matterport.com/) | 3D digital twins, spatial walkthroughs, dollhouse views, and measurement-oriented capture. | A benchmark for premium, spatially rich room inspection. |
| [Kuula](https://kuula.co/) | Hosted 360° panorama tours and embeds. | Could be used by hotels to publish tours; competes with the media layer. |
| [CloudPano](https://www.cloudpano.com/) | 360° virtual-tour authoring and publishing, including real-estate/hospitality use cases. | Adjacent tool hotels or photographers can use instead of a native SpacialStay viewer. |
| [Panoee](https://panoee.com/) | 360° tour creation and hosting. | Lower-friction alternative for creating tours. |
| [3DVista](https://www.3dvista.com/) | Virtual-tour authoring with advanced interactive/VR features. | Competes on tour-building capability rather than hotel booking inventory. |
| [Tourision](https://tourision.com/en/hotels/) | Hotel-focused 3D/360° virtual tours, room-type presentation, floor plans, and info points. | Particularly close to SpacialStay's hotel-room visualization proposition. |
| [Visiting Media / TrueTour](https://visitingmedia.com/) | Hospitality-oriented 3D/360° visual sales experiences and venue workflows. | Relevant for hotel and venue sales experiences. |
| [RoomTour](https://roomtourapp.com/en/for/hotels) | Hotel room 360° capture workflow using a phone and shareable tours. | Shows that capture and publishing can be a product on their own. |

## 3. How SpacialStay can differentiate

1. **Verified room-level media:** each 3D model is tied to a specific room type, floor, view direction, and capture date.
2. **Booking integrated with the tour:** the user moves from the exact room view to live availability and checkout without leaving the flow.
3. **View transparency:** clearly distinguish river/city/garden/blocked/no-window views and disclose when a view is representative rather than guaranteed.
4. **Transparent all-in price:** show taxes, platform fees, property fees, and cancellation terms before payment.
5. **Boutique-hotel focus:** onboard a manageable number of properties with high-quality verified room content instead of trying to list every hotel.
